Spring 2026 · Columbia HPML with IBM Research · team of 5

Uncertainty-aware inference: how quantization changes LLM confidence

Mentored by Ruchi Muhindru, Distinguished Engineer at IBM Research. We measured how four post-training quantization methods (GPTQ INT8, GPTQ INT4, AWQ INT4 and NF4) change Mistral-7B's confidence calibration on TriviaQA, HellaSwag and PubMedQA, then profiled the GPU kernels on an NVIDIA L4. Accuracy alone hid the damage: on PubMedQA, average calibration error improved while worst-case error got much worse. GPTQ INT4 on the Marlin kernel ran 1.78× faster than FP16 under vLLM.
